# Skalpelator and USkalpelator

The two croppers: their sources, their build and how they work with the site. The other documents are listed in the [README](../README.md).

The two croppers are Svelte apps with their sources in `apps/skalpel/` and `apps/uskalpel/`. Vite builds them with the base `/skalpel/` and `/uskalpel/` straight into the site's `skalpel/` and `uskalpel/`, which are committed, so `deploy.sh` sends them like any other file and deletes what a new build left out. After a change to an app (Node.js 22.12 or newer, which Vite 8 needs):

```bash
tools/build-apps.sh            # both, or: tools/build-apps.sh skalpel
```

and its sources go into the commit together with the build. CI builds them again (`npm ci`, so exactly the versions of `package-lock.json`) and fails when the committed build is not the one of the sources. For working on one, `npm run dev` in its folder serves it with live reload; what belongs to the site (its fonts, icons, `account.php`, the gallery) it takes from the site running locally (see [development.md](development.md)), or from the address in `SANAKAN_SITE`. The code both use is in `apps/shared/`; what of it runs without a page (which `blob:` addresses an app lets go of, `ownedUrls.js`, how many decoded pictures the renderer keeps, at most 4 and 256 MB, and the draft of the work, `draft.js`: settings written a moment after a change, a picture only when another takes its place, and all of it before a login link leaves the page) is tested by `node --test apps/*/test/*.test.mjs` with USkalpelator's frames (`apps/uskalpel/src/lib/frames.js`: which pictures of the bot make up each frame) and layers (`apps/uskalpel/src/lib/layers.js`: Scalp kept over the opening of its frame's mask, so nothing behind it shows, and in Obie both layers moved, zoomed and stopped at the edge alike) and Skalpelator's opening (`apps/skalpel/src/lib/opening.js`: the picture stays 448x650 as the bot expects it, but only has to cover, with 2 px to spare, the part all the bot's frames leave open, so it can be moved and zoomed out up to what the frame hides), both on `apps/shared/lib/cover.js`, also in CI. The apps let go of every `blob:` address they made once it is no longer shown and when they go, drop a crop, a file read or a removed background that ends after a newer one was asked for, and keep what the user did when the styles of the frames (`/pw/variants.json`) come late; a failure to get them says so and can be tried again. Their fonts are the site's own (`css/fonts.css`, linked in their `index.html`, its `?v=` kept up to date by `tools/asset-versions.php` like any page's).

Their header is the site's: `apps/shared/lib/Header.svelte` puts in the account menu of every page through the site's own `js/account.js` and `css/account.css` (the account's styles, split from `css/style.css` so the apps can take them without the site's whole stylesheet), linked in their `index.html` with `js/appearance.js`, which gives them the HUD colour the account picked; the search of every page comes the same way, `js/palette.js` and `css/palette.css`, after the app, whose footer gets its button. `account.php?back=<page>` makes logging out come back to the page asking, and also says whether the account has a folder of its own in the gallery (`own`) and its CSRF token; `account.php?login&back=<page>` logs in and comes back there. "Do galerii" (`apps/shared/lib/GallerySave.svelte`) sends the card to `i/` with the action `cropper`, which makes the folder `Skalpelator` (`CROPPER_DIR`) in the account's own folder the first time and from there is an upload like any other, with the same limits. The card goes as a lossy WebP made in the browser (`apps/shared/lib/webp.js`: libwebp in WebAssembly, `@jsquash/webp`, loaded at the first save; quality 98, method 6, sharp YUV, lossless alpha) from the very pixels the PNG of "Pobierz obrazek" has, which the gallery keeps as it is. The answer of every upload carries the file's link and its folder (`uploadedLinks()`). "Z mojej galerii" (`apps/shared/lib/GalleryPick.svelte`) lists the pictures of the account's own folder, subfolders included and newest first, from `i/?pick` (`pickList()`, JSON with each one's link and thumbnail; 401 without a login, 403 without a folder of its own), and crops the one chosen; it is on the site, so the crop reads it without asking another server.

They run in the browser alone, saving into the gallery apart: the pictures of the cards come from the site's own copy at `/pw/` (`inc/pw.php`), and the models of USkalpelator's background removal from the site's `/models/`.

USkalpelator's background removal (`apps/uskalpel/src/lib/background.js`) offers four models, all run on the CPU: IMG.LY's two general ones through `@imgly/background-removal` (Szybki, ISNet quantised, 44 MB; Dokładny, ISNet in fp16, 88 MB), and through onnxruntime itself SkyTNT's anime-segmentation (Anime: ISNet trained on anime characters, Apache-2.0, converted to fp16, 88 MB) and BRIA's RMBG-1.4 (fp16, 88 MB; BRIA's licence allows only non-commercial use). Whichever cut the figure out, the half-transparent edge takes the colour of the solid figure near it instead of the old background's (`apps/uskalpel/src/lib/matte.js`, tested with the rest). The models and onnxruntime's WebAssembly are made by

```bash
node tools/bg-models.mjs       # what is missing; --force for all of it again
```

after `npm ci` in `apps/uskalpel/`: it downloads them (checked against pinned SHA-256 hashes, or IMG.LY's own), converts SkyTNT's to fp16 (Python with `onnx` and `onnxconverter-common`, `PYTHON` picks the interpreter), takes the WebAssembly from the app's `onnxruntime-web`, and cuts every file into pieces of 4 MiB named by their SHA-256 (`.bin`) in `models/`, listed in `models/resources.json`, the format IMG.LY's library reads (its `publicPath`) and the app reads for the other two. `models/` is out of git: `deploy.sh` sends it like the skins' songs, and nginx keeps the pieces for a year and asks for the list every time. `onnxruntime-web` is the app's own version, which `overrides` in `package.json` gives IMG.LY's library too (it asks for exactly 1.21.0), and Vite resolves it to its build without WebGPU (`onnxruntime-web/wasm`, whose WebAssembly is half the size); the build leaves out the copies of the WebAssembly onnxruntime brings (`vite.config.js`). After changing `onnxruntime-web` run `tools/bg-models.mjs` again (it always takes the WebAssembly anew), after changing `@imgly/background-removal` with `--force`, and deploy.

They ran on GitHub Pages at `skalpel.sanakan.pl` and `uskalpel.sanakan.pl` before; those names lead to the site by two redirect rules in Cloudflare (Rules → Redirect Rules, a single redirect each: hostname `skalpel.sanakan.pl`, dynamic target `concat("https://sanakan.pl/skalpel", http.request.uri.path)`, 301, query string kept; the same for `uskalpel`), with their DNS records proxied. The server never sees those names.
