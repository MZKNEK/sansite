# The Safeguard's register

The finds of the site, how the server proves them and how they reach an account and the ranking. The other documents are listed in the [README](../README.md).

More easter eggs, each a find of the Safeguard's register (`js/safeguard.js`, `inc/auth/finds.php`): the 404 page is a place in the Megastructure, a floor, a sector and a stretch of corridor walled up by the Builders, worked out from the address, so a wrong link always leads to the same place; the browser's console greets with the Safeguard's warning, and `safeguard()` there reports to it; the Konami code (on a phone: the arrows as swipes, then two taps) lets a Silicon Creature into the page, which goes green, shakes and crumbles until the Safeguard sweeps it clean; the answers of `account.php` (which every static page asks) and `megastructure.php` carry an `X-Safeguard-Haslo-Awaryjne` header with a password for the terminal, `toha-` and four characters of `SAFEGUARD_SECRET`, typed there as a command; the home page's motto takes Morse keyed on it with the mouse or the space bar (a short press a dot, a long one a dash) and answers in Morse, then in plain text (`SOS`, `SANAKAN`, `KILLY`, `PING` and a few more; anything else gets `POWTÓRZ`); three shots of the beam in a row (each within 15 seconds of the last) overload it: the third goes back across the logo, which splits along it and grows back together, as Sanakan regenerates in the manga; left alone for three minutes, the home page falls asleep, dark and grey, all but the logo, whose eyes glow, until anything is done (`js/home.js`). With firing the beam, the Netsphere's scan, five words of lore in the terminal and being cut off from the network (seeing the offline page, or a page open while the browser loses the network and gets it back, as Firefox's "Work Offline" never asks the service worker) and Killy, a shadow with one white eye and the Emitter charging in his hand, who now and then walks along the bottom of the window a while after a page opens and stops when clicked: on one page in 250 for a browser without finds, each find one more chance in 250, at most one page in 20, and no more once met: the browser rolls five times likelier and he comes only in the slots of 20 seconds of the server's clock that its own roll lets him walk in (one in five, the same for everyone), with a ticket the click needs (`localStorage.setItem('safeguard-killy', '1')` sends him on every page all the same, the find still only with a ticket), those are twelve finds. A few more wait where the site refuses, asks who is there or breaks down; the register hints at them, and these notes leave them at that. Five more are the hardest, one for each fragment of the gene and one for the gene itself, on the way to the Net Terminal Gene, which in BLAME! everyone looks for: at the Builders' hours, from 3:00 to 3:07 at night or, for whoever cannot stay up, from 15:00 to 15:07 (Polish time, the server's clock), a door opens on the 404 page, with the first of the gene's four fragments behind it and a way down that each account walks on its own (only a logged-in account may take any of the way to the gene), to floor zero, with the second fragment and a maze in the dark (arrows, WASD, or a swipe running on to the next turn; only what is near shows, and the page never has the maze whole: after every step `megastructure.php` checks the walk so far and answers with what is near where it ends), whose terminal deep inside, once the server has checked the walk, gives a word for the Safeguard (nothing on the page says how to walk it); `safeguard('<word>')` in the console gives the third fragment, and the fourth the Safeguard leaves only on paper: from then on every page asks for it beforehand and keeps it in memory, using either the locally saved maze word or the logged-in account's recorded third fragment (`maze`), so a new browser needs no cached word; static pages ask again when their account menu arrives. A page printed (its preview is enough) has it as a line on top, which nothing on the screen ever shows; the four together, `gen <sequence>` in the Netsphere's terminal, give the gene, and the Safeguard lets the visitor in (`gen` alone lists the fragments the browser has, kept in `localStorage`). As the repository is public, none of it is in the code: the fragments, the way down, the maze and the word come from `SAFEGUARD_SECRET` in `inc/config.php` (HMAC), and `megastructure.php` gives out each only where it belongs, the door only in its hour by the server's clock; without the secret the gene cannot be found (the preview has its own, and there `document.cookie = 'safeguard-time=03:02'` in the console pretends the Builders' hour, `SAFEGUARD_PRETEND_TIME` in `tools/preview/config.php`, never set on the site). There is one more, which the register counts and shows only as ???; the Safeguard does not speak of it. Some come in their time of the year (`holidays()` in `js/safeguard.js`, `safeguardHolidays()` in `inc/auth/finds.php`): on 31 October Silicon Creatures run along the edges of the page, its cards and frames and the window itself (with reduced motion they only sit there for a while), a click catches one, the motto sends "Cukierek albo psikus" and the terminal knows `halloween`; snow of glyphs falls on every page from 20 December to 20 March, through Christmas and the calendar winter (an account can turn it off in its profile, kept in `inc/data/snow.json` and the cookie `snow`); from 20 December to Epiphany the motto sends "Wesołych świąt" and Sanakan wears a Santa hat, which a click knocks off for the rest of the holidays; on 31 December and 1 January a rocket stands by the logo of the home page and a click sends it up into fireworks with luminous trails (which go off by themselves at midnight on any page open), and the motto sends "Szczęśliwego Nowego Roku"; from Palm Sunday to Easter Monday (Easter worked out for the year) an egg sits on a HUD box of every page anyone may open (the home page, the commands and their changes, the status and the versions, the privacy notice, and the 404 page once) and breaks when clicked, and the motto sends "Smacznego jajka"; on 1 April the final letter of Sanakan glitches between N and the Japanese katakana for n; clicking it switches only the home page to Japanese with a red HUD and teto.webp in place of the logo, including navigation, the account menu, status messages and search, and earns the seasonal find (clicking either the Japanese title or its English SANAKAN subtitle restores Polish, the original logo and the account color; a reload restores the account's saved skin and words setting). The same look is the skin `Teto` — and `Miku`, its teal counterpart (`inc/skin-catalog.php`, `js/skins.js`, `js/home-ja.js`, `css/skins.css`): the find unlocks both in the profile (`/account/`), where the account puts one on for good, with its Japanese words or without them — the site's owner has every skin without the find — and the home page wears it, its colours, logo and words, on any day, while the colours go over the whole site; the pick is kept in `inc/data/skins.json` and in the cookies `skin` and `skinwords`, so the pages wear it before the first paint (`js/appearance.js`, `js/skins.js`); the find of Halloween, the night of the silicon creatures, unlocks `Echidna` and `Marisa`, colours and logos without words; the find of Christmas unlocks `Ceres Fauna` and `Padoru` the same way; the one of Easter unlocks `Reisen` and `Pekora`; the one of the New Year unlocks `Nakiri Ayame` and `Albedo`; on any day, PONG keyed in Morse on the motto opens ping pong against the Safeguard and earns the permanent "Ping pong z Safeguardem" find immediately (mouse, touch or arrow keys/W/S; first to five points); on the bot's birthday, 13 October (it first ran in 2017), a cake with candles in the shape of the digits of its age stands by the logo of the home page, a click blows them out with a burst of glyphs, and the motto sends "Sto lat" (the terminal knows `urodziny` too); on every Friday the 13th a title of the page glitches into glyphs now and then, and a click on it while it lasts catches the glitch. The hat, the rocket, the eggs from five pages, the April letter, the cake, a caught Silicon Creature and a caught glitch are seasonal finds, listed apart in the register and out of its count (`SAFEGUARD_SEASONAL`); the register says how soon one not made yet comes back, and the terminal has lore for each. `localStorage.setItem('safeguard-date', '10-31')` (`'10-13'` for the birthday, `'12-31'`, `'04-01'`) (or `'2027-03-26'` or `'2026-11-13'`, with the year, for Easter or a Friday the 13th) in the browser's console makes the site pretend that day, and `localStorage.setItem('safeguard-idle', '5')` puts the home page to sleep after 5 seconds (`removeItem` ends either); the server gives the find then only in the preview, which skips the days and the waits. Every find needs a proof from the server, its time, a random nonce and the find signed with `SAFEGUARD_SECRET`, so `SanakanSafeguard.found()` in the console or `localStorage` edited by hand gives nothing: the answers of `megastructure.php` that make the finds of the way to the gene carry it, and the others it witnesses as well as it can (`?witness=`, `megaWitness()`): a seasonal find only on its day (Polish time, give or take 14 hours for other time zones), the idle page, the shot and the broken logo only as long after a ticket of when they began (`?ticket=`), Killy only with his ticket, each ticket good for its find once, so passed on it gives nobody another (`inc/data/tickets.json`), the header's password only right; the scan, the console, the Konami code, the lore, the network lost, the Morse code and the 404 are as easy to make as to fake, and their proof only puts the server's time on them. One more the server checks whole before it gives it (`snakeAnswer()`), keeping an account's best result (`inc/data/snake.json`), which the ranking shows. The way to the gene goes in its order: a step counts for an account only after those before it (`megaChainFinds()`), and the maze answers only a walk that carries the proof of floor zero. A proof counts for the first account that sends it (`inc/data/proofs.json`), and every proof given out is a new one, so two visitors in the same second never share one. A find is kept in the browser (`localStorage`) with its proof and goes to the account at once, or after the next login, through `account.php?finds`; the profile lists them in `inc/data/finds.json`, and `/hunters/` ranks the accounts by them and lists, for each secret, who made it and when, the admins and the flagged accounts among them without a place. An account whose new finds could not have been made, the Builders' hour outside it, more than 3 finds within 5 seconds, 5 within 2 minutes or a step of the way to the gene sooner after the one before than it can be made (`MEGA_CHAIN`), is flagged by the Safeguard: listed last in the ranking, without a place, out of who found each secret first, and told so in its profile; the panel's profile of an account (`/admin/?konto=ID`) shows its register, takes back one find or all of them (gone from its browser on its next visit too) and sets or takes off the flag.

The register's account steps (`builders`, `floor0`, `maze`, `paper`, `gene`, `admin`) have their account ID included in the proof's signature. Another account cannot claim even a fresh proof or use its floor-zero proof for the maze. Finds already recorded in `finds.json` remain unchanged; old, unclaimed proofs of these steps must be obtained again because their original owner was not recorded. Anonymous discoveries outside that chain can still be claimed after login. The browser keeps the first pending proof and queues distinct replacements in `localStorage`, so recollecting a step preserves its original order. A rejection applies only to the proof that was sent; queued replacements are tried in register order, and following steps remain pending while an earlier proof is retried. Confirmed or withdrawn finds clear their replacement queue.

Games and ticket redemption use POST with a CSRF token from `megastructure.php?csrf`, also for anonymous players: the session's token for a visitor with a session, otherwise an HMAC of a random `mega_csrf` cookie (HttpOnly, SameSite=Strict), so playing without an account writes no session file. A ticket's issued proof is saved atomically in `tickets.json`; retries return that exact proof, with its original time, and it can still be claimed by only one account. The browser retries a failed write once. Ticket issuance is limited to 60 per address per minute (an IPv6 /64 counts as one address), with HTTP 429 and `Retry-After` when exceeded. The rate store has at most 2048 active addresses and the ticket store at most 10000 entries (`MEGA_TICKET_*` constants); expired entries are removed by their own lifetime, and a full ticket store refuses new entries with HTTP 503 while existing retries remain available. Valid games can still be automated: replay validation and minimum elapsed times do not prove a human played them.
